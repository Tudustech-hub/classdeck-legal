Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

ClassDeck does not collect, store, transmit, or share any of your personal data. Everything stays on your device.

What ClassDeck does not do

  • Does not require an account or sign-up
  • Does not collect personal information (name, email, location, etc.)
  • Does not use analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting services
  • Does not display ads
  • Does not use cookies or similar tracking technologies
  • Does not connect to the internet to send your data anywhere

What ClassDeck stores, and where

ClassDeck stores the information you enter — class schedules, exam and assignment dates, study session history — locally on your own device only. This data is never transmitted anywhere. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, that information is permanently deleted, since no copy exists anywhere else.

Permissions

ClassDeck only requests the minimum device permissions needed for the app to function (for example, local notifications for class or deadline reminders, if enabled). No permission is used to collect or transmit data externally.

Children's privacy

Since ClassDeck collects no data at all, there is nothing to collect from users of any age, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version of ClassDeck adds an optional feature that involves data leaving your device — this page will be updated, and the date above will reflect that. As of the date above, ClassDeck operates 100% locally with zero data collection.
